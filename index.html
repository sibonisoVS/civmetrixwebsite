01 — What it fixes

Work gets built. Then it has to be proved.

Every one of these costs real money, and none of them show up until someone goes looking.

01
“We built it. Nobody measured it.”

Quantities live in a foreman’s head and a wet notebook. By claim day the detail is gone, so it gets estimated down to be safe.

02
“The rate was fine. The productivity wasn’t.”

An item loses money for three months before anyone compares what it cost to build against what it earns.

03
“That variation was never claimed.”

Extra work is instructed on site, done, and forgotten — because nothing connected the instruction to a quantity.

04
“Which certificate has actually been paid?”

Certified is not the same as received. The gap between them is your money, funding somebody else’s project.

05
“The storeman left. So did the fuel.”

Deliveries, issues and stock counts in three different books that never reconcile to each other.

06
“He signed two timesheets that week.”

A worker moves between gangs and appears on both. Payroll pays it, and nobody catches it until the audit.

02 — Who uses it

One record, entered by the person who was there.

Site staff capture what they already know. Everything above them — the costing, the certificate, the board report — is built from that, not re-typed.

Foreman

The day, as it happened

Who worked, on what activity, for how long. Plant hours read off the meter. Fields open one at a time, so a record cannot be half filled.

Storeman

Stock that reconciles

Materials issued against activities. Fuel requested, received, issued and counted in one closed cycle, so a shortfall shows the day it happens.

Quantity Surveyor

Measured, then certified

Take-off against the drawing register, linked to BOQ items. Rate variance per activity. Certificates carrying the measurement behind every quantity.

Contracts Manager

The commercial position

Cost, certified value, cash received and budget read against each other — with the findings written out, not left as four lines on a chart.

Director

Where the money is

Margin, cash position, what is outstanding and how long the client takes to pay. Every reading opens full screen, in one tap.

Everyone

Works on a phone

Captured on site, on whatever device is in a pocket. Nothing to install and no server in an office.
